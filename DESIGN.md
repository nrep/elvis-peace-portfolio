# Design system

## Direction

The portfolio is a proof ledger: an engineered, cool-white working surface where claims and evidence are paired in alternating diptychs. It borrows the discipline of an architecture decision record, not the appearance of a terminal.

## Mode

Experience — visitors encounter the work itself first; interface chrome stays quiet.

## Structure

- Hallmark macrostructure: Split Studio.
- Navigation: N9 edge-aligned, reduced to identity, section index, and one contact action.
- Footer: Ft5 statement.
- Each major project pairs narrative context with a bespoke evidence panel.
- The decision ledger is the sole signature interaction; all states remain visible without JavaScript.

## Palette

- Engineered Paper: `oklch(98.5% 0.004 250)`
- Soft Panel: `oklch(96% 0.008 250)`
- Graphite: `oklch(20% 0.016 260)`
- Primary Ink: `oklch(24% 0.02 258)`
- Secondary Ink: `oklch(38% 0.018 257)`
- Signal Cobalt: `oklch(51% 0.2 256)`
- Hairline: `oklch(87% 0.012 252)`

## Typography

- Display: Space Grotesk, 500–600.
- Body: Inter, 400–600.
- Utility: JetBrains Mono, 400–600, reserved for evidence labels and metadata.

## Motion

One restrained page-load/reveal system and one selection transition in the decision ledger. Reduced-motion users receive the final static state immediately.

## Accessibility

Semantic landmarks, skip link, visible focus, keyboard-operable ledger tabs, 44px minimum controls, contrast-compliant tokens, and responsive layouts at 320, 375, 414, and 768 CSS pixels.
